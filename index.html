Privacy & Policies

Privacy Policy

Mana Hub respects your privacy. We collect personal information only as necessary to provide our services. Your data is protected using industry-standard security measures.

Data Collection

We collect: name, phone number, email, delivery address, location data for delivery tracking, and payment information. This data is used solely to process and deliver your orders.

Location Tracking

With your consent, we collect GPS location data to enable real-time delivery tracking. This data is encrypted and deleted after 90 days. You can disable location tracking anytime.

Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days for cancellations made before delivery. Partially delivered orders are not eligible for refunds unless items are damaged.

Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled within 5 minutes of placement. After that, cancellation depends on delivery partner availability. Cancellation charges may apply.

Delivery Policy

Deliveries are made within the promised timeframe. Delays due to weather, traffic, or unforeseen circumstances may occur. We aim for 99% on-time delivery.

Data Security

All payment and personal information is encrypted using SSL technology. We do not share your data with third parties without consent, except as required by law.

Contact for Privacy Concerns

For privacy inquiries or to request data deletion, contact us at privacy@manahub.delivery or call 7799086877.
